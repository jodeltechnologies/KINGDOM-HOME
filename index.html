Where to start when it feels far gone

She has told you what is missing: your time, your talk, your warmth, and the training of the children. That is not a mystery to solve. It is a list to obey.

Do not start by explaining yourself. Start by asking her one question and then keeping quiet long enough to hear the whole answer:

"I have not given you my time and I know it. I am not here to defend myself. Tell me what the last months have felt like for you, and I will just listen."Say it once. Then listen without interrupting, for as long as she needs.

Then pick one thing from her answer and change it this week. One. Changed things convince; promises do not.

The husband's assignment

Five ways love shows up

Love in Scripture is a verb before it is a feeling. These are the five doors love walks through. Find the one she opens fastest.

Before the five

Ephesians 5:25 does not ask you to feel something. It asks you to give something up.

Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it.Ephesians 5:25, KJV

Christ's love cost Him His schedule, His comfort and His body. So the test of whether you love your wife is not what you feel at 6am. It is what you gave up by 9pm.

Which one is hers?

You are probably loving her the way you receive love. That is why it is not landing. Ask her these three questions and let her answer freely.

  1. What did I do in our first year that made you feel most loved?
  2. When you feel far from me, what would bring you back fastest?
  3. What do you keep asking me for that I keep not hearing?

Note. Her answer to question three is almost always her primary love language. Write it down. Then act on it before the week ends.

Set up

Groq, for writing lines

Two ways to connect. Pick the one that matches how you deployed this.

The safe one. Put GROQ_API_KEY in your Vercel project under Settings, Environment Variables, then redeploy. The key stays on the server and never reaches the phone. Nothing to type here.

Careful. A key typed here is stored in this browser and sent straight from this phone. That is fine on your own phone. Never put it in the code you push to GitHub, and never use this mode on a shared computer. Set a spend limit on your Groq account either way.

Share with her, without a server

The simplest way to keep both phones in step. Save a file here, send it to her on WhatsApp, she opens it under this same button. It merges rather than overwrites, so if you both wrote altar notes on the same day, you keep both.

Why this over Supabase. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to keep awake, and your family’s notes never leave the two phones. Do it once a week, on a Sunday, and you are as in step as you need to be.

Supabase, to share with her

Read this first. Supabase pauses free projects after seven days of low activity, and their own guidance is that it takes a few requests a day to stay out of the pause list. If you go two quiet weeks the sync stops working until you press Resume in their dashboard. The repo ships a GitHub Action that pings it daily to prevent this. If you would rather not manage that, use the file above instead. It does the same job with nothing to keep alive.

Optional. Both phones see the same roster, altar record and notes, automatically. Without it everything still works.

How the code works. Anyone holding the household code can read and write your family's notes, so treat it like a house key. Type the same one on her phone and the two stay in step. Run supabase/schema.sql in the Supabase SQL editor once before this will work.

Put it on your home screen

Android, in Chrome:

  1. Open the three dots at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if it offers that.
  3. Name it Kingdom Home and tap Add. It will sit with your other apps and open full screen.

If Install does not appear. That happens when the file is opened straight from Downloads. Put this one file on any free web host, open the link in Chrome, and the full install option appears. Add to Home screen works either way and is enough for daily use.

Deeper Christian Life Ministry

Founded in 1973 by Pastor W.F. Kumuyi, then a mathematics lecturer at the University of Lagos, out of a fifteen-person Bible study in his flat. A holiness-Pentecostal church, with branches across Cameroon and around sixty other countries. The motto: your spiritual welfare is our concern.

The twenty-two tenets held by the church

Why it matters for your marriage. Marriage is one of the twenty-two. In this church it is taught as lifelong and honourable, a picture of Christ and the church, and the home is expected to keep a family altar. So the neglect your wife has named is not a private disappointment. It is a doctrinal matter for you, and your church already has the framework to help you carry it.

The weekly rhythm

Ask for help. Every Deeper Life assembly has married elders and a pastor who counsel couples, and this is exactly the kind of thing they are there for. Going to them early is not failure. Going late is what makes it hard.

This phone's memory

Everything you type here, your names, her number, notes, roster and streaks, is stored in this browser on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you press one of the buttons above. Clearing your browser data clears this too, so save the file first if you care about the notes.

One honest word

An app cannot repair a marriage. It can only make it harder to forget, and easier to start. The repair itself will happen in unglamorous places: an evening you did not spend on your phone, an apology with no but in it, a Tuesday you came home when you said you would.

Your wife does not need you to become a different man. She needs the man she married to turn up. Start tonight, and let her see it for a month before you ask her to believe it.
